Hello and welcome to my page !

I am Emeric Le Floc'h, a senior staff astronomer working in the Astrophysics division of the IRFU institute at CEA-Saclay (France). The questions I seek to answer relate to galaxy formation and galaxy evolution, which I try to address using distant cosmic explosions. Some of my favorites are the so-called Gamma-Ray Bursts, known as the most powerful phenomena in the Universe after the Big Bang.

  Education

  • HDR, 2014, Université Paris-Diderot: Signature of cosmic star formation across the electromagnetic spectrum
  • PhD, 2003, Université Paris XI: Massive star formation at low and high redshift, probed by luminous infrared galaxies and Gamma-Ray Burst hosts

  Past employment

  • 2006 — 2009: Spitzer Post-doctoral Fellow, University of Hawaii (USA)
  • 2003 — 2006: Research Associate, University of Arizona (USA)
  • 1999 — 2000: National service, European Southern Observatory (La Silla, Chile)
